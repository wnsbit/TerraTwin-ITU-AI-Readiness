TerraTwin

Every decision on your farm, before you make it.

Register what you grow and TerraTwin keeps the irrigation and fertiliser calendar, reads a sick leaf through the camera, warns you when the weather turns, connects you to the growers around you, and lets you rehearse a season in the sandbox before it costs you a real one.

Built on Recommendation ITU-T Y.3172 — the ML pipeline, orchestrator, and sandbox that structure every decision this tool makes.

Submitted to the ITU AI Readiness Hackathon — Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Concept, design and development by Wanas Zakri.

Local mode

Google Gemini API key

Optional, and shared by two features: Farmy's answers and the Plant doctor camera. Get a key from Google AI Studio. When set, questions are sent to Google's endpoint with the TerraTwin knowledge base as grounding context, and plant photos are sent to Gemini vision for diagnosis. About this project

ITU AI Readiness Hackathon

TerraTwin is an AI readiness digital twin for farms in the Jazan region — the Tihama coast, the wadi farmland at Sabya, the Abu Arish plain, and the terraces of Al-Reeth and Fifa. It was submitted to the ITU AI Readiness Hackathon — Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, and its decision architecture implements Recommendation ITU-T Y.3172 node for node.

Waiting

No scan yet. Capture or upload a photo and the analysis appears here — species, life stage, health score, what is wrong, and what to do about it.

How the vision model is wired. The camera uses the same Google Gemini key you set under Settings. With no key the app falls back to an on-device colour analysis that reads chlorosis and necrosis from the leaf pixels — less precise, but it still runs with no network and no account.
